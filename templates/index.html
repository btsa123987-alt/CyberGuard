HOW IT WORKS

Clear insights. No false promises.

CyberGuard examines a URL's structure, including lookalike domains, shortened links, insecure HTTP, unusual ports and account-related words. Each signal contributes points to a transparent score. The engine does not open the link, check its reputation, or claim an AI-based certainty.
